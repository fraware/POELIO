# Community conduct

POELIO is a literary and technical research project. Its collaborative spaces should support rigorous disagreement about writing, mathematics, interpretation and design without personal attacks.

Contributors agree to treat participants and authors with respect, critique work rather than identities, avoid harassment and discriminatory conduct, and respect confidentiality, attribution and the rights of literary creators. Feedback about artistic quality may be demanding and specific; it should remain reasoned and directed at the work.

Do not upload private manuscripts, participant writing, credentials, or identifying research data to public issues or pull requests. Report vulnerabilities privately according to [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Maintainers may moderate comments, close inappropriate discussions or decline contributions that violate these principles. Use an existing private channel to raise sensitive conduct concerns. This concise policy does not claim third-party governance or a previously constituted moderation committee.
