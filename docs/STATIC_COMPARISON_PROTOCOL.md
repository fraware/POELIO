# POELIO — Noninteractive reading comparator and evaluation boundary

**Status:** two usable static explanatory companions exist. They are NOT yet validated as exposure-matched comparison conditions for a causal educational study.

## Source-derived static companion links

Both static companions import the same approved literary source strings as the interactive experience. There is no generated literary prose and no duplicate manuscript ledger:

- /?companion=register — Le Registre des clefs: original literary reading, written account of the incompatible alternation/repetition model and fourteen minima, actual second authored realization, interpretive and creative prompts.
- /?companion=permutation — Les Quatre Témoins: four passages in original order, written explanation of the 24 permutations and precedence-filtered 12, another order of exactly the same four passages, interpretive and creative prompts.

These static pages are readable and printable without using the six-stage interactive navigation. They already communicate the mathematics in prose. **Never give them to editorial reviewers before the first-reading critique**, as doing so contaminates the initial literary encounter.

The static explanations are an editorial alternative, not a deliberately inferior straw-person control. They carry the same original text, actual comparison alternative, and substantively accurate formal facts. They lack the interactive choice among all fourteen/twenty-four configurations and do not claim equivalence of exposure time.

## Two different evaluation questions

**Question 1 — editorial merit.** Does the first-reading prose merit attention independent of the rule? Use the pre-reveal links, independent literary judgment, and recorded criticisms. A static comparison of interactive controls does not answer this question.

**Question 2 — educational contribution.** Does the interactive literary laboratory improve understanding of formal mechanisms, the distinction between computation and interpretation, and independent invention, as compared with an exceptionally good static explanation? A controlled study, not the editorial review, would answer this question.

## Prospective study design, to refine and approve before recruitment

One option is a randomized, counterbalanced experiment with two conditions, **interactive six-stage experience** versus **static companion**. Both see the same initial authored passage and the same source-bound alternative. The facilitator keeps reading time and instructions comparable. Use one work per session; do not let a participant encounter both conditions for that work before outcome measurements.

For a mixed session, alternate assignment by an independently prepared schedule held outside the application; stratify by prior Oulipo familiarity if feasible. The order of two works is another potential confound. Record the pre-specified allocation and deviations; do not silently substitute a person's preferred condition.

Suggested outcomes, decided before a controlled study:

1. **Formal comprehension:** explain the exact structural constraint and the effect of violating or modifying it. Report misconceptions and boundaries of the result.
2. **Epistemic discrimination:** identify which claims follow from the finite model and which express a literary interpretation.
3. **Transfer:** analyze a **new, untrained** example of a different constraint mechanism, ideally scored with a pre-approved rubric by assessors unaware of the assigned condition.
4. **Invention:** articulate a new feasible (or intentionally inconsistent) literary procedure and produce a short authored response; assess using anchored, independent qualitative criteria, preserving disagreements.
5. **Qualitative engagement:** participants describe what they found compelling, confusing, or aesthetically empty, separate from the above outcomes.

Do not use click counts, self-reported enjoyment, or automatically computed constraint satisfaction as proxies for quality of literary invention.

## Study integrity

The previous La Relève blinded manuscript evaluation is entirely separate. Exclude those texts and participants if exposure would undermine its blinding. Preserve informed consent, withdrawal, privacy and researcher role boundaries; consult the relevant ethics/IRB rules before treating observations as human-subject research. The POELIO frontend has no consent management or response collection. Do not imply otherwise.

The small facilitated workshop proposed in docs/WORKSHOP_PILOT.md is an **observational design probe**, not a study powered to claim causal superiority. In a later formal study, specify an analysis plan and feasible sample size and document the static comparator's clarity, time and length equivalence before interpreting outcomes.

## Release boundaries

All companion prose derives from the existing approved *public* fixtures. The private La Relève master archive must never enter the public companion, route, test fixture, CI artifact or static hosting. Human literary reviewers' responses are never included in the Git repository without explicit authorization and redaction.

**Current evidence:** executable model tests and accessible prototype UI. **Not established:** literary excellence, independent reviewer acceptance, human learning effects, or superiority over static reading.
